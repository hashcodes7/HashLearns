# Questions You Can Ask the Recruiter

Choose 2–3 rather than asking all of them.

- [ ] 1. How is the Cloud AI Engineer role positioned between hands-on engineering and customer-facing technical consulting?
- [ ] 2. What does a typical project look like for someone joining this team?
- [ ] 3. How much of the role involves building solutions directly versus helping customers architect and deploy them?
- [ ] 4. What does the interview process look like after this exploratory conversation?
- [ ] 5. For this role, what distinguishes candidates who perform particularly well during the technical interviews?
- [ ] 6. How closely does this team work with the Gemini and Google Cloud engineering teams?

---

# Interview Rule

For every technical answer, try to follow:

**Context → Architecture → Your contribution → Technical decision → Result → Trade-off**

Example:

> "The problem was X. I designed Y architecture. My specific contribution was Z. We chose this approach because A. It improved B, but the trade-off was C."

This is much stronger than simply listing technologies.
