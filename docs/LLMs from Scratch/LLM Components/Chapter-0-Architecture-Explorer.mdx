## How to use the Explorer

- **Expand/Collapse**: Click the arrow on the right side of a node to see its sub-components.
- **Navigate**: Click on any node with an external link icon to jump directly to the lesson for that component.
- **Search**: Use the search bar in the top-left to filter components by name. The tree will automatically expand to show matching results.
- **Pan & Zoom**: Click and drag the background to move around. Use your scroll wheel or the bottom-left controls to zoom in and out.
