# 3.3 Dot Product

With multiple inputs, the weighted sum can be written more compactly.

For two inputs:

$$
prediction=w_1x_1+w_2x_2+b
$$

The part before the bias is the **dot product**:

$$
\boxed{w\cdot x=w_1x_1+w_2x_2}
$$

So:

$$
\boxed{prediction=w\cdot x+b}
$$

---

## 3.4 More Inputs

For three inputs:

$$
w\cdot x=w_1x_1+w_2x_2+w_3x_3
$$

For $n$ inputs:

$$
\boxed{w\cdot x=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_ix_i}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{prediction=w\cdot x+b}
$$
